# Frame OS TV

Frame OS TV is the television-focused product in the Frame OS ecosystem.

## Target platform

**Android TV only** for the initial project.

Jio TV and other television platforms are not confirmed targets for this project.

## Project status

**Planning**

This repository currently contains planning and documentation. It does not represent a completed TV operating system or a released application.

## Product direction

The goal is to explore a modern, television-friendly software experience that is practical to use from a distance and with a TV remote.

The exact product form is still to be decided. Research will determine whether the best approach is an Android TV application, a launcher-style experience or another supported implementation.

## Initial areas of investigation

- Television home-screen and interface concepts.
- Remote-control navigation and focus handling.
- Readability and accessibility from typical viewing distances.
- Application and platform integration.
- Performance on supported Android TV hardware.
- Compatibility across selected Android TV versions and devices.
- Privacy, permissions, updates and reliability.

## Development stages

1. Research Android TV platform capabilities and restrictions.
2. Define product requirements and supported devices.
3. Compare technical approaches and choose a stack.
4. Design interface and navigation prototypes.
5. Build and test a small proof of concept.
6. Review results before committing to full implementation.

## Testing

Testing devices and Android TV versions have not yet been finalised. Do not assume that every Android TV device or Google TV device will be supported until compatibility requirements are documented.
